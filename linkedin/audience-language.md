# Audience language (template)

Voice-of-customer research: how your readers actually talk about the problem you teach. Every LinkedIn post, landing page and email should sound like a reply to one of the quotes you collect here. Fill it in once, refresh it every quarter or when engagement dips.

## How to build it

1. **Pick the forums your readers post in.** For business owners that is usually Reddit: the general small-business and entrepreneur subreddits, the subreddit for their trade, and one or two for the topic itself (for example the AI subreddits if you teach AI). Aim for 8 to 10.
2. **Find the threads.** Search Google for `site:reddit.com/r/<sub> <your topic> <a pain word>` ("overwhelmed", "where to start", "worth it", "scam") and keep 30 to 50 threads with real discussion.
3. **Pull the comments.** A Reddit scraper (for example Apify's `trudax/reddit-scraper-lite`) on those thread URLs, a few hundred to a thousand comments in all.
4. **Filter for signal.** Keep comments that state a feeling, a failed attempt, a question in the reader's own words, or advice that got upvoted. Drop jokes, links and arguments about tools.
5. **Group into themes** (usually 5 to 8) and write each one up below. Quote verbatim, attribute by subreddit only, never by username. Keep the quotes in your private copy; don't publish them.

## Who is reading

Describe your readers as one or two people, in a few lines each: what they run, what they feel about the topic, what they're afraid of. Often it is the same person at two moments (for example, "hasn't started yet" and "tried it, it didn't stick").
