## Never

- Greet or introduce yourself at the top ("hey guys", "welcome back", "my name is"), unless that's genuinely you.
- Corporate or formal words: leverage, synergy, utilize, furthermore, moreover, additionally, "deep dive", "in this video we will explore".
- Hype words: game-changer, unlock, revolutionary, seamless, supercharge, 10x, thrilled, incredible.
- Curiosity openers: "Have you ever", "What if", "Did you know". "Here's how / what..." handoffs. "Stop..." openers.
- Engagement gates and hard sells ("smash that like button", "don't forget to").
- Fake authority, or a result you can't show.
- In anything written: em dashes, curly quotes, hidden characters, stacked one-line zingers. `system/schedule.py` refuses copy that has them, using `linkedin/human/slop.json`.

## Hook template

A hook in your voice:

1. Opens the way you open (a receipt, or your usual first line). No greeting.
2. Hits one specific number, dollar figure or time saved in the first 15 seconds.
3. Has one humble beat.
4. Says what the video will show.
5. Can be said in one take, to camera, no props.
6. Lands between 25 and 40 seconds (about 60 to 100 words at your pace in tone.md).
